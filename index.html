How it works

Fully done for you. You don't have to learn new software or change how your office runs.

1

We learn your practice

Your hours, services, insurance you take, how you greet patients, and what counts as an emergency. Ringwise is set up to sound like your office.

2

It answers when you can't

Calls forward to Ringwise only when your team doesn't pick up, or during the hours you choose. Your front desk stays in charge.

3

Your team gets every lead

Each call ends with a booked appointment or a clear text summary: who called, why, and how to reach them.

Two ways to run it

You decide how much Ringwise handles.

Book appointments

Ringwise books new patients straight into open slots you've set aside, so they're on the schedule before they hang up.

Capture and text

Prefer to keep full control of your schedule? Ringwise takes the patient's details and texts them to your team, so you can call back first thing.

Questions

Does this replace my front desk?

No. It's for the calls your team can't physically get to. Your front desk keeps answering like they always have.

What if we want to control our own schedule?

Use capture-and-text mode. Ringwise doesn't book anything. It takes the caller's details and texts your team.

Will it sound like a robot?

It sounds natural and answers with your practice name and your details. The best way to judge is to hear it, which is why we start with a demo.

What about patient privacy?

We'll walk through exactly how call information is handled and stored on our call, before anything goes live.

Do we have to change our phone system?

Usually not. In most cases your existing line forwards unanswered calls to Ringwise. We'll check how your phones are set up on the call.
