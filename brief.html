Questions

Where does this come from?

County health departments in North Carolina post their inspection reports on a public state portal. We read those reports every week. Every row in your list links to the original record so your team can check it before they call.

Are the quotes edited?

No. The inspector's comment is copied exactly as written. We add the sorting, the flags and a suggested opening line, and those are labeled as ours.

Most of these places already have a pest company. Why call them?

Because the record shows the current service isn't holding. The brief flags repeat violations and the cases where the inspector asked for more frequent treatment. Those are the accounts most likely to listen.

Is a citation proof of an active problem?

It's a record of what the inspector saw on that date. Some issues are fixed the same day. Open the linked record before you reach out, and quote it accurately.

Which counties do you cover?

North Carolina counties that post to the state's public inspection portal. Tell us where you work and we'll confirm before you pay anything.

Are you connected to the health department?

No. Ringwise is an independent business. We read public records and have no relationship with any health department or restaurant.
